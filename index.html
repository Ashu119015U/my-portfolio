01 Background

About me

Developer by craft, leader & founder by vision.

Originally from Asansol, West Bengal, I completed my schooling at Loyola School, Taldanga and moved to Hyderabad to pursue a B.Tech in Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence at Newton School of Technology (St. Mary's Rehabilitation University).

Alongside engineering, my peers and faculty entrusted me to serve as the Class Representative (CR) of our batch — coordinating academic initiatives, student affairs and communication across the cohort.

As co-founder of Elkara, I bridge technology and business — turning deep tech into practical products: tamper-proof attendance hardware, a central university OS, and speech AI assistants.
